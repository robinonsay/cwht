Context: the lead SE asked whether three reviews that had used their three allowed iterations, each with one reviewer-specified Major fix left (ADR-056 record INSP-130/131; WP-PDR-20a frequency budget INSP-056/111; WP-PDR-23a sequencer timing INSP-122), could have a fourth, offering (1) a fourth round for these three, (2) the same plus standing permission when the reviewer has written the fix, or (3) no fourth round. Recorded (lead SE reading; the owner may correct it):

- Plan rule C1 ("At most three iterations before escalation to the owner") is struck for the PDR work now; the next plan revision records it. The software plan (`docs/process/07-software-engineering-plan.md` section 10.2) says to escalate to the owner after three author-review iterations; this statement is the owner's standing answer to every such escalation ("continue until APPROVED"), so the baselined text needs no change for the work to go on. Removing the sentence from 07 is carried to the next change request that touches 07.
- The record schema's `iteration` maximum of 3 stays; further passes are filed as "iteration 3 re-issue N", as before.
- First effects: the fourth iterations of INSP-130/131 (ADR-056), INSP-056/111 (WP-PDR-20a) and INSP-122 (WP-PDR-23a), and a fourth impact review iteration of CR-019 in place of carrying its two Majors to S1 as conditions (CR-019 section 6.9 item 3 is overtaken).
