## 1. Raw simulation files and the push to GitHub

The presenter stopped a push of main before it reached GitHub (remote main unchanged at `3a5f5ec`). The 41 unpushed commits carried 1.17 GB of LTspice `.raw` waveform files (238 files, the largest 94.6 MB, under GitHub's 100 MB per-file limit), admitted by the `.gitignore` negations of `57eacc9` (2026-09-27). No review record lists a `.raw` blob in its `product_files`. The presenter offered three options:
- A: push as it is (a public repository of about 1.3 GB, permanent), and from now on keep `.raw` files over 5 MB out of git, on the owner's Mac, with a SHA-256 fingerprint of each committed (recommended);
- B: rewrite the 41 commits to remove the raw files, which changes every commit id that records and documents cite;
- C: Git LFS, which also rewrites history and exceeds the free quota (about USD 5 a month).

Owner statement, verbatim: "Lets go with A"

Recorded:
- main is pushed as it is.
- From now on a `.raw` file over 5 MB is not committed. It stays in its results folder on the owner's Mac, and the run commits a `raw.sha256` manifest (`shasum -a 256` format, checkable with `shasum -c`) naming each such file with its size, so a later re-run can be shown identical. A local pre-commit hook rejects any staged `.raw` over 5 MB.
- The `57eacc9` negations depart from 05 section 10.1, which lists LTspice `.raw` and `.log` outside `docs/vv/` as gitignored derived data. That departure is `docs/cm/deviations.md` entry 6. The retention rule (small `.raw` and `.log` files committed with their results, files over 5 MB kept locally with a manifest) goes into 05 by a Class II CR, with its impact reviews before the owner's disposition.
- The owner's answers to the receiver filter escalation (INSP-117, rule C1) and to TS-012 revision 6 with a further INSP-110 iteration are still open.

## 3. Correction to section 1 (manifest wording)

Section 1 says the `raw.sha256` manifest lets "a later re-run ... be shown identical". The CR-017 author found that every LTspice `.raw` file starts with a `Title:` line naming the run's temporary deck path and a `Date:` line with the run time, so a whole-file SHA-256 identifies the kept file but cannot show a re-run identical. A re-run is shown equivalent by an equal deck SHA-256 and equal result numbers and checker verdict (CR-017 C2 and its Q4). The local hook was also narrowed to exclude `docs/vv/`, where 04 section 15 already admits a cited `.raw` up to 10 MB (CR-017 H1).
