### REQ-TX-013: Synthesizer frequency sample for the independent frequency verification

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Statement | The transmitter shall output a 3.3 V sample below 20 MHz whose frequency times a fixed ratio is within 1 kHz (TBR) of the carrier. |
