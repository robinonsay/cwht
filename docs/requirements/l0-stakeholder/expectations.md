## 2. Need, Goals and Objectives

### NGO-001 (Need): Pocket true-CW 2 m handheld for Morse contacts among friends

**Statement.** The owner and a small group of licensed friends need a pocket-sized handheld radio for the 2 m amateur band that lets them talk to each other by true CW (A1A) over simplex paths, using either a straight key or iambic paddles with headphones, that is lawful and safe to operate under 47 CFR Part 97, and that works at first power-on because every risk has been closed before the boards are built.

**Rationale.** The problem is social and technical at once: the owner wants to play radio with friends by Morse (SI-019) on a band where CW gives a propagation advantage over tone-modulated FM handhelds (SI-004), in a form factor that goes in a pocket (SI-001). The reference-design survey (docs/research/2m-cw-transceiver-reference-designs.md F1 to F5) finds no pocket 2 m CW handheld with a built-in keyer for both key types among the products it covers: the Mizuho MX-2 (F3) is the closest precedent, a historical 200 mW SSB/CW handheld with VXO coverage, and the other radios surveyed are portables rather than pocket handhelds (F1, F2, F4, F5). The owner therefore designs and builds one to the core requirement of SI-018. Assumptions: the owner and every friend hold at least a Technician license (SI-030); the radios are built in a run of at most five units and are never marketed (SI-035, 47 CFR 15.23 context in docs/research/part97-regulatory-basis.md F9).

**Parent:** -. **Sources:** SI-001, SI-003, SI-004, SI-005, SI-010, SI-014, SI-018, SI-019, SI-030. **MOEs:** -. **Status:** Draft.

### NGO-002 (Goal): Reliable simplex CW contacts over useful ranges at 5 W

**Statement.** Two radios in the hands of two friends complete CW contacts over open ground at several kilometres and from a hilltop to the horizon, anywhere in the US 2 m band, using the pocket antennas the radios ship with.

**Rationale.** SI-003 chose 5 W for useful simplex range; SI-024 asked for the full 144.000 to 148.000 MHz band. docs/research/antenna-and-erp.md F11 shows that at 5 W over open flat ground the CW link is horizon-limited (10.1 km at 1.5 m antenna heights) for every realistic whip except a bare quarter-wave on a table, and that a line-of-sight hilltop path has 38 to 59 dB of margin; the antenna and clutter, not power, set the range. Assumes the reference antennas of docs/research/antenna-and-erp.md ANT-05 (a quarter-wave flexible whip with a 48 cm counterpoise as the pocket reference; an end-fed half-wave as the range reference).

**Parent:** NGO-001. **Sources:** SI-003, SI-004, SI-019, SI-024. **MOEs:** MOE-001, MOE-002, MOE-010. **Status:** Draft.
