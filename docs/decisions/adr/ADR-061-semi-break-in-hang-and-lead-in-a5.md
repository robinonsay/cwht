## 1. Context

ADR-026 (Accepted, 2026-09-26, SRR decision 47) restated ADR-010 and fixes semi break-in only, an operator-set hang of 3 to 30 dits (TBR) at the displayed speed with a default of 8 dits, the same lead-in of at most 12 ms (TBR) for every element of an over, a relay-class T/R element with cold switching, and a T/R element trade study at PDR. The owner's A5 decision of 2026-09-29 (ADR-056 section 2 item 1; `docs/decisions/trade-studies/TS-012-design-to-cost-hand-built.md` section 10) keeps the mode, the hang values, the lead-in, the relay clause and the cold-switching sequence, and changes two clauses of ADR-026 section 2 (ADR-056 section 7 item 4):
- **Hang reference.** ADR-026 counts the hang "at the displayed speed" and "in dits of the displayed WPM". A5 has no display (TS-012 descope D1); a Morse-code audio menu replaces it (TS-012 section 8.7). TS-012 section 8.10 changes REQ-SYS-044 from "Hang time in dits at the displayed speed" to "At the set speed", and REQ-SW-KEYER-032 from "Hang of 3 to 30 dits of the displayed speed" to "Of the set speed", both carried by CR-018. The hang values do not change.
- **T/R element.** ADR-026 says "The T/R element itself (HF3 versus other relays versus a PIN switch used only in semi break-in) is chosen by the T/R element trade study at PDR". That study (TS-008) is not written, because TS-012 decides it (ADR-056 section 2 item 3, TS-008 row): the Omron G5V-2, a DPDT relay that is not RF-rated, with an RX-grounding pole, its isolation measured on the NanoVNA before first transmission (TS-012 sections 8.1 and 8.3 row 2; descope D9; exception EX-14).

An Accepted ADR is not edited (README rule 2; `docs/process/05-configuration-and-data-management.md` Table 4-1 row 13), and rule 2 has no partial supersession. The lead SE ruled on 2026-09-29 (ADR-056 section 7, ruling (c)) that ADR-026 follows ruling 2, the precedent that ADR-026 itself set when it restated ADR-010 and superseded it in full. This ADR therefore restates every unchanged part of ADR-026 verbatim or near-verbatim, states each A5 change with its source, and supersedes ADR-026 in full on the S1 disposition.

ADR-026's context, restated: ADR-010 recorded the owner's semi break-in decision (SI-036) and added hang and lead-in values that the requirement authors had since replaced. ADR-010 section 2 gave a hang default of 8 dits with 6.1 dits and a fixed 50 to 2500 ms selectable, and a first-element lead-in of at most 12 ms. REQ-SYS-044 and REQ-SW-KEYER-032 gave an operator-set hang of 3 to 30 dits (TBR) at the displayed speed (at the set speed for A5, above), REQ-SYS-136 keeps 8 dits as the default, and REQ-SYS-161 gives the same lead-in of at most 12 ms (TBR) for every element of an over. The independent review INSP-011 (finding F-04) recorded the conflict, and ADR-026 restated all of ADR-010 with the aligned values.

## 2. Decision

Rev A implements semi break-in only. While sending, the operator hears the sidetone and not the band. After the last key-up the radio returns to receive after an operator-set hang time of 3 to 30 dits (TBR) at the set speed, default 8 dits (REQ-SYS-044, REQ-SYS-136). The hang counts in dits of the set WPM in every keyer mode, Straight included. **(Changed for A5: TS-012 descope D1; section 8.10 rows REQ-SYS-044 and REQ-SW-KEYER-032.)** ADR-026 counted the hang at the displayed speed. A5 has no display, so the hang counts at the set speed: the keyer speed the operator sets with the Morse-menu item S, which the status read-out announces in Morse (TS-012 section 8.7; REQ-SYS-060 via CR-018). There is no fixed-millisecond hang option and no 6.1-dit preset. Full QSK is excluded from rev A and is not a configuration option.

The T/R element may therefore be a sequenced relay, with its normally-closed contacts to the receiver so the unpowered state is receive, with cold switching enforced by the sequencer: T/R to transmit, wait for operate plus bounce, PA bias on, raised-cosine rise; on key-up the envelope falls completely, bias off, guard, T/R to receive after the hang. **(Changed for A5: ADR-056 section 2 item 3, TS-008 row; TS-012 sections 8.1 and 8.3 row 2; descope D9; exception EX-14; D-5.)** The T/R element is the Omron G5V-2 (G5V-2-DC5), a DPDT relay that is not RF-rated, in place of ADR-026's example of a TE Axicom HF3 class RF relay (1 Form C) and of the T/R element trade study at PDR, which is not written. Pole A switches the antenna path between transmit and receive, and pole B grounds the receiver input in transmit. Its coil is held at reduced power by PWM after pull-in, or the G5V-2 high-sensitivity coil is fitted if its price at the gate allows (D-5). Its isolation and loss are measured on the NanoVNA before first transmission (EX-14). The sequence values (ramp start at least 10 ms after the relay command, the frequency check finished before PA_EN, the clamps held and the integrator parked until ramp start) are set by WP-PDR-23 (TS-012 section 8.12, WP-PDR-23 row).

Every radiated element of an over is delayed by the same lead-in of at most 12 ms (TBR) after the receive-to-transmit changeover (REQ-SYS-161), and the RF rise begins within 15 ms (TBR) of each straight-key contact closure (REQ-SYS-160). The harmonic low-pass filter sits at the antenna port after the T/R node, and the LNA supply is off during transmit. **(Changed for A5.)** In TS-012 section 8.1 the 7-pole LPF sits between the G5V-2 pole A and the antenna SMA, which meets the first clause. TS-012 section 8.1 does not draw the LNA supply in transmit; it adds the pole B ground on the receiver input. WP-PDR-23 shows the second clause with the sequence (cross item).

| B | ADR-010 values: default 8 dits, 6.1 dits and a fixed 50 to 2500 ms selectable; first-element lead-in only | Not recommended (as ADR-026): a 50 ms fixed hang releases the relay between elements below 24 WPM, which is per-element switching that SI-036 excludes and the relay wear case of `tr-switch-candidates.md` F5 |

| E | Relay operated in full QSK | Rejected: relay life 24 to 794 h of keying (F5) |

No trade study for the break-in mode: the owner's direction leaves option A as the only viable mode, and the alternatives are recorded per SE HB §6.8.1.2.2, as ADR-026. The class 1 status of this choice is stated in the header. **(Changed for A5.)** The T/R element, class 1 (a) and (b), is no longer a PDR trade: TS-012 chose it (ADR-056 section 2 item 3).
