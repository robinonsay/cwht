## 1. Context

ADR-019 (Accepted, 2026-09-25) records the owner's ruling that every new peripheral driver cwht needs is developed upstream in rustos, not in a cwht-local crate (SI-033). Its section 2 says the drivers are "delivered as reviewed pull requests that the owner merges as rustos maintainer", and its assumption 1 says "The owner, as rustos maintainer, merges the work-package pull requests on the schedule". Under ADR-019 the owner's merge was the act that approved rustos code for cwht (CR-019 section 12 Q3). By 2026-09-29 five driver packages (WP-SW-11, 09, 01, 02 and 03; ADR-051 to ADR-055; reviews INSP-095 to INSP-106) were reviewed and waited for her merge into rustos `master` (OD-23 at S1; status note 2026-09-29 section 9).

On 2026-09-29 the owner directed otherwise (section 6 transcribes her words). The same evening the lead SE created the branch `cwht/develop` at `48e07ec`, the top of the reviewed stack, and pushed it to `git@github.com:robinonsay/rustos.git`. Its base, the owner's local `master` commit `2ec64c0`, became public with it; `origin/master` is unchanged at `c54d35a`, and no merge request was opened (status note section 9). After the owner's correction, rustos work is done on `cwht/*` branches of her local repository, in the linked worktree `~/rust/rustos-cwht` on `cwht/develop`, because her own checkout `~/rust/rustos` holds staged and untracked tutorial changes (same section). CR-019 pins cwht to the fixed commit `48e07ec` on `cwht/develop`, with no merge before the pin, and makes the owner's OD-37 disposition of that CR her approval of the reviewed code (CR-019 section 5 step 1; section 12 Q3). The WP-SW-08 ICD page has since been reviewed (INSP-132 and INSP-133) and pushed onto `cwht/develop` at `00d5383` (CR-019 section 6.9 ruling 4).

An Accepted ADR is not edited (README rule 2; `docs/process/05-configuration-and-data-management.md` Table 4-1 row 13), and rule 2 has no partial supersession. ## 2. Decision

Every new peripheral driver cwht needs is developed upstream in rustos: the trait in `rustos/api`, the implementation in `rustos/firmware/pico2` with a datasheet citation on every register access, contract tests, and a dev-board check.

**(Changed, the owner's direction of 2026-09-29.)** Claude develops this cwht-driven rustos work on `cwht/*` branches of the owner's local rustos repository, working in its linked worktree `~/rust/rustos-cwht`, and pushes the branches to `git@github.com:robinonsay/rustos.git` as the work goes, starting with `cwht/develop`. Each work package goes onto `cwht/develop` after its review. Claude never force-pushes or rewrites a pushed rustos branch, because the cwht lock pins commits on it, and never changes the owner's own checkout (`~/rust/rustos`) or its uncommitted files. Reviewed rustos code enters cwht only when a pin-move CR names a fixed commit on `cwht/develop` (05 Table 4-1 row 26; the lock Rule cell of CR-019 section 1 item 1); a commit added to the branch later enters only by another pin-move CR. This holds for a flashed image through two rules that this record relies on and does not change. `firmware/Cargo.toml` resolves rustos by path to the owner's checkout (`../../rustos`), not to the pin, so every build, audit and gate for the record reads a `git archive` export of the pinned commit and never that working tree (the clean-export rule of the lock row, unchanged by CR-019 section 1 item 1). And only tagged releases from `firmware/releases/`, with a version description (version and hash), are flashed, and every version description records the rustos commit used (lock row Rule cell), with `picotool verify` after every flash (HZ-014 K5; REQ-SYS-132, REQ-SYS-143). The owner's disposition of each pin-move CR is her approval, as rustos maintainer and decision authority, of that code entering every cwht image. It accepts no work-package ADR: each is accepted after its own design review (CR-019 section 5 step 1). The merge of this work into rustos `master` stays the owner's, by a merge request she submits later. It is a separate act and does not move the cwht pin by itself.

The work packages are those of 07 section 19 (clocks and PLL, TIMER0 alarms, NVIC and critical sections, I2C, SPI, ADC, PWM, watchdog, flash store, UART, and optional PIO I2S), each run as a sprint of 07 section 3.4 with the file inventory of 3.5 and proposed as its own ADR in cwht when its trait shape is fixed. cwht-local code is limited to `cwht-core` (application logic), `cwht-app` (target binary), `cwht-hal-mock` (host implementations of the traits) and `firmware/emu` (scenarios); no cwht-local HAL or register crate exists. cwht consumes rustos as a path dependency with the commit recorded in the lock file and in every VDD (the CM plan's OQ-5 default; conversion to a git dependency or submodule is decided at PDR). No SVD-generated peripheral access crate is used.
