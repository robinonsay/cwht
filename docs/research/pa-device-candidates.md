# 144 MHz 5 W power-device candidates for a 2S (6.0 to 8.4 V) supply (assignment: pa-devices)

Date: 2026-09-25. Author: Claude (research subagent). Status: research report, not a baseline. Extends `2m-cw-transceiver-reference-designs.md` section E, Table 3, risks 6 to 8, decisions 16 and 18, open items 4 and 9.

## Question

Establish a current-production baseline for the 5 W-class 144 MHz power device that a turnkey assembler (PCBWay) can obtain, for a two-cell Li-ion supply of 6.0 to 8.4 V. For each candidate: lifecycle status with a dated source; datasheet URL and revision; package and thermal pad; gain and drain efficiency at 144 to 175 MHz; rated output; second and third harmonic levels at rated power where the datasheet gives them; matching data (large-signal impedances or a reference circuit); SPICE or S-parameter model availability; price and stock at DigiKey, Mouser, RF Parts, Arrow and the Mitsubishi authorized channel with the date and time observed; and whether PCBWay could source it or the owner must consign. Cover at minimum Mitsubishi RD07MUS2B and the current 7.2 V RD parts, NXP AFT05MS006N and AFT05MS004N, NXP AFIC901N, and at least three other families. Derive the low-pass filter attenuation needed at 288 and 432 MHz for a 60 dBc target from the best-supported harmonic data, the output droop across 6.0 to 8.4 V with the ALC or regulation options, and recommend a baseline device and a fallback with the sourcing path for each.

## Method

1. Read the prior report (`2m-cw-transceiver-reference-designs.md` F22 to F25, Table 3, open items 4 and 9), the Part 97 report (`part97-regulatory-basis.md` F2) and the PCBWay report (`pcbway-fabrication-and-assembly.md` F17, F18) so that this report extends rather than repeats them.
2. Re-fetched 47 CFR 97.307 from the eCFR versioner API at issue date 2026-09-23 (`curl -sL --compressed "https://www.ecfr.gov/api/versioner/v1/full/2026-09-23/title-47.xml?part=97&section=97.307"`, 2026-09-25 16:39 UTC) and re-derived the suppression numbers with a short Python script (arithmetic in Finding 12).
3. Downloaded the primary datasheets with `curl` into the session scratchpad (`.../scratchpad/pa/`) and converted them with `pdftotext -layout`; the text was searched with `grep` and the tables were read from the text. Files: Mitsubishi RD07MUS2B (publication Jan 2024, document code G2K-Si-240116-1), RD08MUS2 (Apr 2024), RD10MMS2 (Jan 2026), RD12MVS1 (Jan 2026), RD02MUS2 (Jan 2026), RD01MUS2B (Dec 2016), RA07M1317M (Jun 2019), Mitsubishi Silicon RF Devices catalog H-CX624-Y KI-2506 (new publication effective Jun 2025) plus the older RFMW-hosted copy, NXP AFT05MS006N (Rev 0, 2/2014), AFT05MS004N (Rev 0, 7/2014), AFIC901N (Rev 0, 1/2016), Guerrilla RF GRF5604 (Release 0), Ampleon product catalog (April 2026), Modelithics ADS library brochure, and Mitsubishi application note AN-VHF-053-A "RD01MUS2 & RD07MUS2B RF characteristics data at 135 to 175 MHz (Vdd = 7.2 V)" (6 Dec 2010, rev 8 Mar 2011; 19 pages; retrieved from the Internet Archive copy because Mitsubishi's site no longer links it).
4. Graph pages that pdftotext cannot tabulate (RD07MUS2B page 4 typical characteristics; RA07M1317M pages 3, 4 and 8) were rendered with `pdftoppm -r 110 -png` and read visually. Numbers taken from a graph are labelled "graph read" and carry about +/-5 % (RD07MUS2B) or +/-2 dB (harmonic curves) reading uncertainty.
5. Lifecycle status was taken from vendor product pages fetched on 2026-09-25 (Mitsubishi Electric silicon RF device list, Mitsubishi Electric US product pages, NXP product pages, Renesas product page). Distributor stock and price were taken from the OEMsTrade aggregator (`https://www.oemstrade.com/search/<part>`) between 2026-09-25 16:45 and 16:57 UTC because DigiKey, Mouser, Arrow, Octopart, TrustedParts, Findchips detail pages and TME returned HTTP 403 or timed out for automated fetches; the DigiKey keyword-search endpoint answered for some part numbers and those answers are cited separately. Only rows flagged "authorized" (ECIA/NEDA member) are used as evidence of a legitimate channel; broker rows are reported only to show that grey stock exists. RF Parts, Anglia and Mitsubishi Electric US pages were fetched directly.
6. WebSearch was used for discovery only; the session search budget was exhausted before the Infineon, Microchip and Wolfspeed sweep, so that sweep rests on the Ampleon catalog, the MACOM search result and general knowledge (marked Low confidence).
7. Derived quantities (LPF attenuation, droop, DC and thermal budgets) were computed with Python from the tabulated datasheet and application-note data and are labelled "derived".

## Findings

### A. Regulatory anchor (re-verified)

**F1. 47 CFR 97.307(e), eCFR issue 2026-09-23, fetched 2026-09-25 16:39 UTC.** Text: "The mean power of any spurious emission from a station transmitter or external RF power amplifier transmitting on a frequency between 30-225 MHz must be at least 60 dB below the mean power of the fundamental. For a transmitter having a mean power of 25 W or less, the mean power of any spurious emission supplied to the antenna transmission line must not exceed 25 uW and must be at least 40 dB below the mean power of the fundamental emission, but need not be reduced below the power of 10 uW." Derived: at 5.0 W (37.0 dBm) the 25 uW (-16.0 dBm) cap binds at 53.0 dBc; at 6.0 W it is 53.8 dBc; at 7.2 W (the RD07MUS2B typical) it is 54.6 dBc; the 10 uW floor corresponds to 57.0 dBc at 5 W. The 60 dBc design target adopted in `part97-regulatory-basis.md` (REQ-candidate RF-04) is used throughout this report. Source: https://www.ecfr.gov/api/versioner/v1/full/2026-09-23/title-47.xml?part=97&section=97.307

### F. Derived: low-pass filter attenuation for a 60 dBc target

**F16. Harmonic data quality ranking.** (1) RA07M1317M: guaranteed maxima 2fo -25 dBc, 3fo -30 dBc at 6 W (datasheet limit, F8); typical -37 / -63 dBc at 144 MHz (graph read). (2) RD01MUS2 + RD07MUS2B line-up in the Mitsubishi evaluation circuit, one lot, measured: at 5 W in-band 2fo -37 to -40 dBc and 3fo -62 to -65 dBc; worst points in the whole data set 2fo -34 dBc (135 MHz, gate control) and 3fo -50 dBc (135 MHz, Vgg 2.2 V); during a gate-controlled ramp at 135 MHz the 2fo ratio reaches -17 dBc but at only 0.3 W, i.e. an absolute second-harmonic level of +7.9 dBm, and the worst absolute level in the ramp is +8.5 dBm at Vgg 2.2 V (F4). (3) AFT05MS006N, AFT05MS004N, AFIC901N, GRF5604, PD54008L-E: no vendor harmonic data.

**F17. Required filter attenuation (derived).** With a 60 dBc target at 5 W (spur not above -23 dBm):
- From the guaranteed RA07M1317M maxima: 60 - 25 = **35 dB at 288 to 296 MHz** and 60 - 30 = **30 dB at 432 to 444 MHz**. For the legal minimum only (53 dBc): 28 dB and 23 dB.
- From the measured RD07MUS2B line-up at 5 W in-band: 60 - 37 = 23 dB at 2f and 0 dB at 3f (already -62 dBc); from the data-set worst case: 26 dB and 10 dB.
- From the absolute-power view of a gate-controlled keying ramp (worst raw 2f of +8.5 dBm at 135 MHz): 24.5 dB keeps every instant under 25 uW, 28.5 dB under the 10 uW floor, 31.5 dB under -23 dBm. Under drive-level (Pin) control the worst raw 2f absolute level is about 0 dBm, so 23 dB suffices for -23 dBm.
- For devices with no vendor data a raw single-ended class-AB stage is assumed at 2fo -15 dBc and 3fo -20 dBc (assumption, Low confidence): 45 dB and 40 dB.
Recommended design goals: **40 dB at 288 MHz and 35 dB at 432 MHz (worst case over 144 to 148 MHz, at the assembled filter's terminals, including the 5 dB implementation margin for inductor self-resonance and layout leakage flagged in `part97-regulatory-basis.md` RISK RF-1), with passband insertion loss not above 0.5 dB at 148 MHz** (0.5 dB costs 11 % of 5 W). These goals cover the guaranteed module case, the gate-ramp absolute case, and the no-data devices at the assumed levels; they do not cover the no-data devices if their raw 2fo is worse than -20 dBc, which is why the baseline must be a device with measured harmonics. A 7th-order Chebyshev or a 5th-order elliptic low-pass with a 165 to 170 MHz cut-off reaches 40 dB at 288 MHz on paper; the filter design itself is PDR work and is not done here.

### G. Derived: output droop across 6.0 to 8.4 V and control options

**F18. Droop.** Measured (F4, 155 MHz, Pin 30 mW, Vgg 3.5 V): 5.7 W at 6.0 V, about 7.8 W at 7.2 V, about 10.0 W at 8.4 V (interpolated), a spread of 2.4 dB with total efficiency constant at about 55 % and Idd rising from 1.72 A to about 2.15 A; the V-squared rule of the prior report (2.9 dB) is slightly pessimistic because the stage compresses at high VDD. RD07MUS2B alone (F3, graph read, Pin 0.3 W): 4.5 W at 6.0 V to about 9.4 W at 8.4 V (3.2 dB). RA07M1317M (F8, graph read): 5.3 W at 6.0 V to 10.5 W at 8.4 V (3.0 dB), exceeding its 10 W maximum rating near full charge at full drive. Consequences: an unregulated design set for 5.0 W at 7.4 V gives about 3.3 to 3.5 W at cell cut-off and 6.4 to 6.8 W at full charge; a design set for 5.0 W at 6.0 V gives 8.5 to 9.8 W at 8.4 V, which exceeds the 3 W carry rating of the G6K-2F-RF relay class considered for T/R switching (prior report F28), raises the harmonic absolute levels by up to 3 dB, and pushes the 25 uW cap from 53.0 to 55.9 dBc.

**F19. DC and thermal budget at 5 W (derived).** Two-stage RD line-up at 5 W: 11.4 W DC (1.59 A at 7.2 V, F4), about 1.9 A at 6.0 V; the final alone at ηD 55 to 65 % dissipates 2.7 to 4.1 W, a junction-to-case rise of 7 to 10 C at 2.5 C/W (RD07MUS2B), 3 to 4 C at 1.0 C/W (AFT05MS006N), 12 to 18 C at 4.4 C/W (AFT05MS004N); the RA07M1317M module dissipates about 7.9 W at 6.5 W output (14.4 W DC) and needs the case below 90 C. A 3000 mAh 2S pack therefore supports roughly 1.5 h of continuous key-down at 5 W (derived, ignoring receiver and controller drain), which is adequate for CW duty cycles of 30 to 50 %.

**F20. ALC and regulation options (derived from F4, F8).**
(a) None: accept the 2.4 to 3.2 dB spread and write the requirement as 5 W +1.5/-1.8 dB; simplest, but the T/R switch, LPF and harmonic budget must be rated for the 8.4 V power.
(b) Gate-bias (Vgg) control: Mitsubishi's own power-control method for both the discrete line-up and the module; at 7.2 V, Po moves from 4 W to 6 W over Vgg 2.7 to 3.2 V (about 4 W/V at 135 MHz, 3 W/V at 155 MHz), so an RP2350 PWM through an RC filter (or a small DAC) into the Vgg node with a directional-coupler detector feeding an RP2350 ADC input closes a loop that also serves as the keying envelope shaper (raised-cosine on Vgg). Penalty: 2fo degrades toward class C at low Vgg (-33 dBc at 4.9 W, 135 MHz), which the 40 dB filter goal absorbs.
(c) Drive-level (Pin) control: hold Vgg at 3.5 V and set Pout by the driver (a PWM-controlled PIN attenuator or the driver's own gate bias); 5 W needs +7 to +9 dBm at the driver input and 2fo stays at -32 dBc or better at every drive level; cleanest harmonics, one more control element.
(d) Drain regulation by a buck converter to about 5.5 V: the line-up gives 4.9 W at 5.5 V (F4), constant across the pack, at the cost of a 2 A switcher next to the receiver and about 10 % conversion loss; the converter must be disabled on receive.
(e) Linear drain modulator (QRP Labs AN005 style, prior report F31) clamped to 6.0 V: constant 5.7 W (F4) and spur-free, combines with envelope shaping, but dissipates (Vbatt - 6.0 V) x 1.7 A, up to 4 W in the pass element at 8.4 V during key-down.
Recommendation in Implications: (b) with the coupler and ADC loop as the baseline, (c) as the alternative if harmonic margin proves short; (d) and (e) only if the requirement demands a fixed 5.0 W +/-0.5 dB.

## Implications for cwht

1. **REQ-candidate (PA output and tolerance).** Transmitter output at the antenna port shall be 5.0 W +1.0/-1.5 dB (3.5 to 6.3 W) over 6.0 to 8.4 V supply and 144.000 to 148.000 MHz, or, if closed-loop ALC is adopted (F20 b/c), 5.0 W +/-0.5 dB above 6.4 V and not less than 3.5 W down to 6.0 V. Verification: Analysis (behavioural PA model, F6) then Bench (dummy load and RF detector; a wattmeter is not on the owner's bench, Open item). [F18]
2. **REQ-candidate (harmonic filter).** The transmit low-pass filter, measured at its own terminals, shall provide at least 40 dB at 288 to 296 MHz and 35 dB at 432 to 444 MHz with insertion loss not above 0.5 dB at 148 MHz; the assembled transmitter shall meet 60 dBc (spur not above -23 dBm) at every instant of the keying envelope. Verification: Analysis (S-parameter simulation with vendor inductor models and SRF), Bench (NanoVNA S21 of the filter; tinySA Ultra or borrowed analyzer for the transmitter). [F16, F17]
3. **REQ-candidate (PA drive and bias interface).** The PA shall expose a gate-bias control node driven by the RP2350 (PWM plus RC, or DAC) with a range that spans Po 0 to full output, and a forward-power sample (directional coupler plus detector) into an RP2350 ADC channel; keying envelope and ALC share this node. [F20 b]
4. **REQ-candidate (power-path ratings).** Every element after the PA (LPF, T/R switch, antenna connector) shall be rated for the full-charge, full-drive output of the chosen device (10 W for the RD line-up or the module at 8.4 V) unless the ALC is shown by analysis to limit power under all single faults. [F18]
5. **DECISION-needed (baseline device, recommendation).** Baseline: **Mitsubishi RD07MUS2B final with RD01MUS2B driver**, copied from AN-VHF-053-A and the datasheet EVB, direct 2S drain, gate-bias ALC and envelope. It is the only 7.2 V discrete in mass production with measured VHF harmonic, gain, efficiency and impedance data and with a commercial nonlinear model. Sourcing: consigned (MEUS or Richardson RFPD quote for the 25-piece -601 pallet plus RD01MUS2B, or RF Parts NOS bought domestically). Alternative worth a quote at the same time: RD08MUS2, which Mitsubishi now recommends for VHF but which has no published VHF data (would need the owner to accept an in-house match with no vendor harmonic reference). [F2 to F7, F21]
6. **DECISION-needed (fallback device, recommendation).** Fallback: **NXP AFT05MS004N** on its datasheet 136 to 174 MHz reference circuit, sourced turnkey from Newark or Mouser while stock lasts, with a lifetime reserve (for example 100 pcs at about $4.21) bought by the owner immediately if this fallback is retained; it is EOL, so it must not become the published open-source baseline unless the Mitsubishi channel fails. [F8, F10, F21]
7. **DECISION-needed (drop).** Remove AFT05MS006N from the candidate list: no authorized stock anywhere on 2026-09-25 and no VHF reference circuit; broker stock is a counterfeit risk incompatible with the charter's evidence rule.
