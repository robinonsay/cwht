# PA device follow-up under SI-028: turnkey-sourceable 5 W devices for 144 to 148 MHz (assignment: pa-turnkey)

Date: 2026-09-25. Author: Claude (RF research subagent). Status: research report, not a baseline. Extends `pa-device-candidates.md` (F13, F14, F21, Implications 5, 6, 17; Open items 2, 3, 5, 6, 9) under the owner's new constraint SI-028 ("Power amplifier device must be readily sourced from DigiKey, Mouser or PCBWay's turnkey distributors; no bespoke or consignment-only parts", `docs/requirements/l0-stakeholder/stakeholder-inputs.md`). PCBWay's named turnkey distributors are Digi-Key, Mouser, Farnell element14 (Newark), Arrow and Avnet (`pcbway-fabrication-and-assembly.md` F17); Verical is Arrow's authorized marketplace and is counted under Arrow.

## Question

Under SI-028 the Mitsubishi RD07MUS2B line-up recommended in `pa-device-candidates.md` (consigned, no catalog stock) is excluded. Which current-production 3 to 8 W device usable from the 2S pack (6.0 to 8.4 V) or from a regulated 5 V rail at 144 MHz is stocked today at DigiKey, Mouser or another PCBWay turnkey distributor, with enough vendor matching and harmonic data that the transmitter can work at first power-on (SI-010)? Specifically: (1) STMicroelectronics PD54008L-E, (2) NXP AFT05MS004N, (3) Guerrilla RF GRF5604 and siblings, (4) any other stocked device from the NXP, Ampleon, Qorvo, CEL, Infineon, Microchip, MACOM, ST, Toshiba, Mitsubishi, ASI and Microsemi catalogs, (5) a 12 V PA rail from a boost converter enabling stocked 12.5 V devices. For each: SI-028 fit, matching-data maturity, harmonic-filter implication, ALC compatibility, unit cost, risk; then a primary and a fallback that both pass SI-028 and what closes the choice at PDR.

## Method

1. Re-read `pa-device-candidates.md`, `pcbway-fabrication-and-assembly.md` F17/F18, `power-tree-and-charging.md` F17/F18/F22/F23 (PA rail, 5 V bus, current model) and `tr-switch-candidates.md` F7 (HF3 relay rating), and the local regulatory corpus `docs/references/md/regulatory/47cfr-97.307.md` (eCFR 2026-09-23).

ST documents were therefore taken from Internet Archive copies of the st.com PDFs (`web.archive.org/web/2024id_/https://www.st.com/resource/...`, fetched 2026-09-25 17:36 to 17:39 UTC) and ST product and evaluation-board pages were read through the r.jina.ai page reader (a rendered copy of the live st.com page, 17:36 to 17:41 UTC). Every ST number below is quoted from those PDFs after `pdftotext -layout`; graph pages of the STEVAL-TDR003V1 data brief were rendered with `pdftoppm` and read visually (marked "graph read", about +/-0.3 dB, +/-0.05 A, +/-5 dB on the filter plot).
3. NXP product pages were fetched directly (17:29 to 17:33 UTC). Guerrilla RF: the datasheet PDF (Release 0) and the 2026 Product Selection Guide (`20260526_GRF_Vertical_PSG_Online.pdf`) were downloaded directly; the product page was read through r.jina.ai (the site returns HTTP 403 to direct fetches).
4. Stock and price: DigiKey keyword pages answered for some parts (17:29 to 17:33 UTC) and are cited where they did; Mouser, Newark, TME, Avnet and DigiKey detail pages returned HTTP 403 or bot challenges, so distributor rows were taken from the OEMsTrade aggregator (`https://www.oemstrade.com/search/<part>`, distributor identified from the page's `data-distributor_name` attribute) at 2026-09-25 17:31 UTC (PA devices), 17:36 UTC (converters), 17:39 to 17:44 UTC (drivers, evaluation boards). Only rows from ECIA-member authorized distributors are used as evidence; broker rows are mentioned only to show that grey stock exists.
5. Derived quantities (filter notch frequencies, DC and thermal budgets, boost-converter penalty, Part 97 arithmetic) were computed with Python from the quoted numbers and are labelled "derived".

## Findings

### A. Regulatory anchor (unchanged)

**F1.** 47 CFR 97.307(e) (eCFR 2026-09-23, local copy `docs/references/md/regulatory/47cfr-97.307.md` line 25): between 30 and 225 MHz any spurious emission must be at least 60 dB below the fundamental, except that a transmitter of 25 W or less must keep every spurious emission at or below 25 uW and at least 40 dB down, and need not go below 10 uW. Derived at 5.0 W (37.0 dBm): the 25 uW cap binds at 53.0 dBc, the 10 uW floor is 57.0 dBc, and the project's 60 dBc design target is a spur at or below -23.0 dBm. These numbers match `pa-device-candidates.md` F1 and `part97-regulatory-basis.md` RF-04.

**F15. Fit notes (derived).** A 5 V, 2.5 A PA rail is required; the power tree's planned 5 V synchronous buck (`power-tree-and-charging.md` D-PWR-06) is sized for the Pico 2 and audio and would need a 3 A stage with overshoot held below 5 % (absolute maximum 5.25 V) and a minimum-input of 6.0 V against a 5.0 V output (1 V headroom, fine for a buck). At 5 W (1.5 to 2 dB below PSAT) the expected PAE is about 50 %, so the DC input is about 10 W and the dissipation about 5 W, most of it in stage 2 at 20 C/W: a junction rise of about 80 C, Tj about 140 C at a 60 C package base, inside the 190 C limit. Keying: VSHDN switches in microseconds, too fast for the 5 ms raised-cosine envelope, so envelope shaping and ALC must act on the drive (exciter level or a PIN attenuator), option (c) of `pa-device-candidates.md` F20; VCC control (3.6 to 5 V gives 3 to 6 W PSAT) is a coarse power-step option. The 144 to 148 MHz match is unpublished (fractional bandwidth 5 to 10 % means a 460 MHz tune cannot be reused); harmonics are unknown, so the prior report's no-data assumption (2fo -15 dBc, 3fo -20 dBc, needing 45 and 40 dB of filtering) applies until Guerrilla RF supplies data.

**F19. Converter penalty (derived).** For 5 W at the antenna with a 12.5 V device at 55 % (PD55008-E datasheet typical at 500 MHz) to 65 % (optimistic at 144 MHz) drain efficiency plus a 0.9 W driver, the PA rail draws 8.6 to 10.0 W; at 92 to 94 % conversion efficiency (estimate from the TPS61088 loss elements; the datasheet does not show this operating point) the pack supplies 9.1 to 10.9 W, 1.5 to 1.8 A at 6.0 V, with 0.55 to 0.87 W lost in the converter. This is comparable to the direct 7.2 V path (9.6 to 10.6 W, F9); the boost does not buy efficiency, it buys a fixed rail (no droop, 5.0 W +/-0.5 dB by design, drain-voltage power control possible through the feedback divider), a 3x higher load impedance (about 14 ohm versus 4.7 ohm, a lower-loss, more tolerant output match), and 40 V-class device ruggedness. Costs: about $5 to $6 of converter parts per unit (IC $2.05 to $2.74, a 2.2 to 4.7 uH inductor rated 6 A, ceramic capacitors), 2 to 3 cm2 of board, an 18 W switcher at 500 kHz to 2.2 MHz on the same board as a -140 dBm receiver (it must be off in receive and pre-charged before key-down; its ripple appears as AM sidebands on the carrier and its harmonics as birdies, the same class of risk as R-PWR-03 in `power-tree-and-charging.md`), a soft-start delay in the T/R sequence, and, for PD55008-E or PD55015-E, a $12 to $19 device with no vendor 12.5 V VHF circuit (PD55015-E has a 20 V one).

## Implications for cwht

1. **DECISION-needed (primary device, recommendation): STMicroelectronics PD54008L-E, run directly from the protected 2S pack at 6.0 to 8.4 V, with the final-stage match, VAPC bias network and elliptic low-pass filter copied from STEVAL-TDR003V1 and re-tuned from 135 to 175 MHz to 144 to 148 MHz.** It is the only 7 V-class device at or above 5 W that is both in volume production and stocked at MOQ 1 by two PCBWay turnkey distributors, and it is the only SI-028-compliant candidate with a complete vendor VHF reference design and system-level harmonic data (-70 dBc max at 5 W). Owner decision pending at SRR; the device choice becomes a constraint requirement traced to SI-028 and this report. [F2, F5, F8, F16]
2. **DECISION-needed (fallback, recommendation): Guerrilla RF GRF5604 on a dedicated 5.0 V, 3 A rail**, with drive-level ALC and envelope shaping. It passes SI-028 (Mouser, 7,409), removes the driver stage (36 dB gain), costs less, and Guerrilla RF offers custom-tune support; its weaknesses (no published 144 MHz tune, no harmonic data, 5.25 V absolute maximum) are all closable by one applications request and one evaluation build before PDR. The 12.5 V boost path (PD55015-E first, PD55008-E second) is the reserve if Guerrilla RF does not supply a VHF tune; it should not be the fallback because it adds an 18 W switcher to the receiver board and $17 to $29 per unit for no efficiency gain. [F12 to F15, F17 to F19]
3. **DECISION-needed (drop).** Remove NXP AFT05MS004N (EOL, fails SI-028), AFT05MS003N, AFT05MS006N, AFIC901N and all Mitsubishi RD and RA parts from the candidate list; keep AN-VHF-053-A only as a harmonic reference for a 7.2 V class-AB VHF stage. [F10, F11, F16]
4. **REQ-candidate (device constraint, L1 or L2 TX).** "The transmitter power amplifier shall use a device that, at CDR and at the procurement date, is in the manufacturer's active production status and is stocked at minimum order quantity 1 by DigiKey, Mouser, Farnell element14, Arrow or Avnet." Rationale: SI-028. Verification: Inspection (dated distributor and manufacturer pages in the CDR package). [F8, F16]
5. **REQ-candidate (output power with ALC).** 5.0 W +/-0.5 dB at the antenna port over 6.4 to 8.4 V and 144.000 to 148.000 MHz, not less than 4.0 W at 6.0 V (tbr, close by PDR: the 6.0 V figure rests on a graph read of the STEVAL curves at 10 dBm drive). Rationale: F5 shows 4.5 to 5.0 W at 6 V. Verification: Analysis (behavioural model) then Bench. [F5, F9]
6. **REQ-candidate (harmonic filter).** The 40 dB at 288 to 296 MHz and 35 dB at 432 to 444 MHz goals with insertion loss at or below 0.5 dB stand (prior report Implication 2); the design basis is the STEVAL-TDR003V1 7-element elliptic topology with the traps re-tuned; add "at least 40 dB from 288 MHz to 1.5 GHz" so that inductor self-resonance (the -15 dB region near 1.5 GHz in Figure 9) cannot let a high-order harmonic exceed 97.307(e). Verification: Analysis with vendor inductor models, then Bench (NanoVNA, tinySA Ultra). [F1, F5]
7. **REQ-candidate (PA control interface).** Gate-bias node (VAPC-equivalent, 0 to 5 V, driven by RP2350 PWM through an RC filter) plus a forward-power sample into an RP2350 ADC; unchanged from the prior report, now anchored to a vendor circuit that uses the same node. For the fallback the same requirement is met by a drive-level control element. [F5, F15]
8. **REQ-candidate (PA rail ratings).** Elements after the PA remain rated for the full-charge, full-drive output; for PD54008L-E the 8.4 V output is not characterized by ST (board maximum 15 V), so the ALC and a firmware limit must bound it and the Analysis must show the bound under single faults (prior Implication 4 unchanged). [F5]
9. **RISK-candidate (single stocked source, old part).** PD54008L-E is a 2007 part from a vendor that has already put its two VHF boards and the driver on NRND or obsolete status; an ST NRND notice would leave cwht with the fallback only. 10. **RISK-candidate (no LTspice model).** The ST model is ADS-only. ## What must happen at PDR to close the choice

1. Behavioural PA model built and correlated to STEVAL-TDR003V1 data (Analysis evidence, SI-010).

3. Driver selected with a gain budget (F20) and the PA control interface (Implication 7) shown to reach 5.0 W at 6.4 V and to limit power at 8.4 V.
4. Guerrilla RF tune and harmonic data received (or refused) and the fallback ADR written either way.
5. Dated distributor and manufacturer status re-checked for PD54008L-E and GRF5604; parts and lifetime reserve ordered.
6. PCBWay confirmation of turnkey sourcing and PowerFLAT assembly.
7. Owner decision memo recording primary, fallback and the drop list (Implications 1 to 3) and the CR that replaces the Mitsubishi baseline of `pa-device-candidates.md`.
