These artifacts are Record class, so they are corrected by errata and not rewritten.

**Why the deck is corrected here and not in its source.** The deck at `64e53ee` is the record of what was presented at the SRR session (decision memo "Slide deck presented"; minutes line 3). Its APPROVED record INSP-029 (`checklists/srr-deck.md`) names `srr.adoc@adca6f79`, `srr.html@c80f62d3` and `srr.css@da8d9463` as product blobs. Any edit to `srr.adoc`, even a comment line, would change that blob. `tools/validate_docs.py` would then fail the record on its drift rule, and the presented record would no longer match what was shown. Deck errata are therefore recorded in this file, and the PDR deck carries the lesson (WP-PDR-50).

**Effect on rulings.** No erratum here changes a ruling. The owner's rulings are those transcribed in `docs/reviews/SRR/decision-memo.md` section 8 from `decisions-for-owner.md` at `5ebe90c`, and they stand as transcribed. An erratum corrects a statement of fact made around a ruling. It does not correct the ruling itself.

**Verification.** Lien L-4 (RFA-SRR-004, `docs/reviews/SRR/rfa-rid-log.json`) asks for each item to be fixed in its product and verified by an independent reviewer before the PDR readiness declaration. Each entry names the reviewer it needs. The review secretary does not verify its own entries.
