**How to use it.**

1. **Key decisions for discussion (part 1).** At most 21 entries (17 in revision 6); each has a real trade-off, a safety or cost consequence, a contested recommendation, or needs a capacity only Robin holds (ETA, SMA TA, HMTA, CIO/SAISO designee, risk taker, rustos maintainer, Configuration Control Board). Each entry is ruled on its own: *yes* to the recommendation, another option, or no answer.
2. **Consent agenda (part 2).** Decisions whose recommendation can be adopted as a block. The owner may say "adopt the consent agenda", optionally "except items N, M", and the excepted items are discussed like key decisions. Every item keeps its recommendation and its if-no-answer assumption.
3. **No answer.** A left item takes the assumption in its last column, which is what every SRR product already says; the assumption is recorded in the SRR decision memo as the owner's ruling only if the owner says so (charter section 2: an approval in chat is transcribed into the memo). An item whose assumption is "not adopted" or "stays open" leaves the risk, TBR or finding named there open. Items marked *PDR* or later are asked now so the owner can confirm the plan; they are ruled at that gate.
4. **Owner actions (part 4)** are things only Robin can do by hand or by correspondence; they are not rulings.
