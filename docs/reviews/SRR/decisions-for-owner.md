**How to use it.**

1. **Key decisions for discussion (part 1).** At most 15 entries; each has a real trade-off, a safety or cost consequence, a contested recommendation, or needs a capacity only Robin holds (ETA, SMA TA, HMTA, CIO/SAISO designee, risk taker, rustos maintainer, Configuration Control Board). Each entry is ruled on its own: *yes* to the recommendation, another option, or no answer.
2. **Consent agenda (part 2).** Decisions whose recommendation can be adopted as a block. The owner may say "adopt the consent agenda", optionally "except items N, M", and the excepted items are discussed like key decisions. Every item keeps its recommendation and its if-no-answer assumption.
3. **No answer.** A left item takes the assumption in its last column, which is what every SRR product already says; the assumption is recorded in the SRR decision memo as the owner's ruling only if the owner says so (charter section 2: an approval in chat is transcribed into the memo). An item whose assumption is "not adopted" or "stays open" leaves the risk, TBR or finding named there open. Items marked *PDR* or later are asked now so the owner can confirm the plan; they are ruled at that gate.
4. **Owner actions (part 4)** are things only Robin can do by hand or by correspondence; they are not rulings.

**Count:** 117 numbered decisions. 15 key decisions for discussion cover 45 of them; the consent agenda holds 70, grouped by theme (decisions 3, 29, 33, 41, 76 and 85 moved from the consent agenda to K4, K12, K2, K1 and K7 after the independent readiness review of 2026-09-26, because each meets the key-decision test); 2 need no ruling (decision 5 withdrawn, decision 12 done). No number is reused or renumbered; the index at the end gives the location of every number.

**Ids used below.** Research decision ids are the reports' own (RFX-D1, D-KN2, D-PWR-03, D-UI-01, D-PCB-06, D-TR-2, DECISION-6 and so on); open questions are OQ-<AREA>-NNN (charter section 6); requirement ids are REQ-<MOD>-NNN in `docs/requirements/`; INSP-NNN are the review records in `docs/reviews/SRR/checklists/`.

## Part 6. Where the rulings go

- Every ruling is transcribed into `docs/reviews/SRR/decision-memo.md` (template `docs/templates/decision-memo.md`) with the owner's wording and date; a block adoption of the consent agenda is transcribed item by item with the words "adopted with the consent agenda". The RMM and compliance approvals (items 4, 6, 7, 8, 9) go into memo sections 7 and 7.1 line by line.
- Decisions that constrain later work become ADRs the same day (charter section 11 rule 6): ADR-015, ADR-022 and ADR-023 change from Proposed to Accepted or Rejected; ADR-027 records decision 107; new ADRs are written for the operating-while-charging rule, the stuck-key control set, the audio level policy, the power-tree baseline, the firmware design rules and the clock plan.
- Requirement values ruled here close their TBR through the plan in each `tbr` object (14 L1 TBRs close in the SRR memo, section 13 of the package); a ruling that changes a requirement before `baseline/srr` is a Log change with the owner's approval, after it a `CR-NNN`. A declined decision 38, 39, 40 or 112 retires the named requirements (02 section 11.3).
- Red risk plan approvals (item 14) are written into each risk's `mitigation.plan_approval` naming the SRR memo and date.
- CR-001 (item 108) gets its disposition block filled from the ruling; the disposition block is the decision memo for that change (charter section 4 item 4).
