# SRR Decisions for the Owner

**Review:** SRR (with MCR). **Prepared:** 2026-09-25 by Claude (lead systems engineer) for Robin (Decision Authority, ETA, SMA TA). **Record of evidence:** `docs/reviews/SRR/package.md` section 13.1 carries the same numbered list with the source ids of every item; this file is the standalone view, grouped by theme, for ruling in one sitting.

**How to use it.** For each numbered item the owner answers *yes* to the recommendation, picks another option, or leaves it. A left item takes the assumption in the last column, which is what every SRR product already says; the assumption is recorded in the SRR decision memo as the owner's ruling only if the owner says so (charter section 2: an approval in chat is transcribed into the memo). An item whose assumption is "not adopted" or "stays open" leaves the risk or TBR named there open. Items marked *PDR* or later are asked now so the owner can confirm the plan; they are ruled at that gate.

**Count:** 104 decisions in 15 themes, deduplicated from the DECISION-needed items of the 22 research reports and the owner decisions and open questions of the charter, SEMP, process documents, ConOps, concept description, hazard analysis, ADR index, technology assessment and TPM file. Items 103 and 104 (theme O) were added at the readiness check of 2026-09-25: CI-10 of 02 section 14 had been missed, and item 104 is the deviation path for entrance row 25. 13 further items are already closed by binding owner decisions and are listed at the end.

**Ids used below.** Research decision ids are the reports' own (RFX-D1, D-KN2, D-PWR-03, D-UI-01, D-PCB-06, D-TR-2, DECISION-6 and so on); open questions are OQ-<AREA>-NNN (charter section 6); requirement ids are REQ-SYS-NNN in `docs/requirements/sys/requirements.json`.

## Where the rulings go

- Every ruling is transcribed into `docs/reviews/SRR/decision-memo.md` (template `docs/templates/decision-memo.md`) with the owner's wording and date; the RMM and compliance approvals (items 4, 6, 7, 8, 9) go into memo sections 7 and 7.1 line by line.

- Requirement values ruled here close their TBR at PDR through the plan in each `tbr` object; a ruling that changes a requirement before `baseline/srr` is a Log change with the owner's approval, after it a `CR-NNN`.
- Red risk plan approvals (item 14) are written into each risk's `mitigation.plan_approval` naming the SRR memo and date.
