### finding-1

<a id="finding-1"></a>**finding-1, Major, Open.** ADR-022 section 2: "The ALC design target holds 5.0 W within plus or minus 0.5 dB above 6.4 V so the absolute cap does not tighten at full charge; the requirement it serves, REQ-SYS-012, is plus or minus 1 dB (TBR, closing at PDR with TS-003 and TS-006)." ADR-060 section 2, marked "(Changed for A5: TS-012 section 8.10 row REQ-SYS-012; D-9, D-10.)": "The ALC holds the selected step within the REQ-SYS-012 tolerance as CR-018 sets it, 5 W +1/-1.5 dB at the 6.4 V end (TBR), with the low-pack setpoint limit of 4.0 W at 6.4 V (D-10), so that the absolute cap does not tighten at full charge."

1. **The source changes the 6.4 V end only.** TS-012 section 8.10 row REQ-SYS-012 changes the requirement "at the 6.4 V end", because A5 gives 4.09 W nominal and 3.19 W at the lowest corner there. D-10 limits the setpoint at 6.4 V. Nothing in TS-012, `pa-drive-ts012.md` or `keying-ts012.md` says that the A5 loop cannot hold the step within plus or minus 0.5 dB at higher pack voltages. The keying analysis's own model-health check is "Top power within 0.5 dB of the (pack-limited) setpoint". ADR-060 nonetheless replaces the design target at every voltage with the requirement tolerance, which is plus or minus 1 dB above the 6.4 V end. At full charge (8.4 V) the decided bound goes from +0.5 dB to +1 dB.
2. **Three hazard controls state the removed bound.** In `hazards.json` 0.5.0-pha:
   - HZ-001 K4: "holds the selected step within +/-0.5 dB from 6.4 to 8.4 V, so the full-charge output cannot exceed the 5 W step by more than 0.5 dB";
   - HZ-003 K4: "caps the output at the selected step within +/-0.5 dB from 6.4 to 8.4 V, so dissipation does not rise with pack voltage";
   - HZ-008 K3: "holds the selected step to +/-0.5 dB from 6.4 to 8.4 V".

   ADR-060 section 4.3 routes K3 and both K4 controls to "ADR-056 section 4.3". ADR-056 section 4.3 re-reads each of them only by "the REQ-SYS-012 delta, 5 W +1/-1.5 dB at the 6.4 V end (TBR)". After S1, ADR-022 would be superseded, and no record would state the full-charge bound that the three controls rely on.
3. **The owner is not told.** The section 6 memo wording says only "The power at the low battery end follows the power requirement change you are asked to confirm with A5". It does not say that the full-charge ALC target is loosened.

A restatement that changes a hazard-control value beyond what its source changes, without putting the change to the owner, is the case that INSP-130 finding-1 rated Major (requirements that include hazard controls shown as changed when the study keeps them). The checklist item is H1: a difference with an Accepted ADR, with both sentences quoted.

**Fix (either):**
- (a) Restate ADR-022's design target and mark the A5 change only at the low end. For example: the ALC design target holds the selected step within plus or minus 0.5 dB above the low-pack region, and at the 6.4 V end REQ-SYS-012 is 5 W +1/-1.5 dB (TBR) with the 4.0 W setpoint limit (D-10). Name the voltage where the setpoint limit ends, or say that WP-PDR-21 or 22 sets it.
- (b) Cite a source that withdraws the plus or minus 0.5 dB target at full charge. Then route HZ-001 K4, HZ-003 K4 and HZ-008 K3 to WP-PDR-16 with the new full-charge bound, check it against the RF-exposure and thermal records that use it, and add the change to the section 6 memo wording in plain terms.

The SWE-134 and mission-critical reading of the ALC unit is for the INSP-135 pair (cross item X-7).

### New finding (iteration 2)

<a id="finding-11"></a>**finding-11, Minor, Open (CK-DES-H1).** The finding-1 fix added a summary of `pa-drive-ts012.md` revision 3 (Draft) to ADR-060, and it misplaces the D-9 conflict:

1. **Where the conflict is.** ADR-060 section 2 (line 45) says the record "finds that D-9 as specified cannot both hold the 8 W open-loop ceiling and reach 3.97 W at 6.4 V". The record says something wider:
   - R3-1: "D-9's two criteria conflict at 8.4 V, whatever the clamp's shape";
   - R3.6: "With D-9 as specified, the basis never reaches 3.97 W at any pack voltage. Its lowest point is 2.07 W at 8.4 V, so a delta would have to be about 5 W +1/-4.0 dB over the whole pack range".
2. **An option left out.** Section 2 gives the extent of the low-pack region as "the 6.4 V end in TS-012, up to 7.8 V in scenario B". Section 4.3 says the three ALC controls change "at the low-pack end only". The memo says "only at the low battery end does it follow the power requirement change". R3-1 names three options for WP-PDR-22, and option (b), "keep 8 W and accept REQ-SYS-012's shortfall over the whole pack range", is not a low-pack-end change. ADR-060 rightly fixes none of these, but the owner reads "only at the low battery end" as settled.

The hazard meaning is not affected. Every option leaves the upper bound, the "+0.5 dB" that HZ-001 K4, HZ-003 K4 and HZ-008 K3 rely on, where finding-1's fix puts it, and a reach shortfall lowers power. So this is Minor.

**Fix:** in section 2, say what the record says: the two D-9 criteria conflict at 8.4 V, and with D-9 as specified the lowest corner reaches 3.97 W at no pack voltage. Add option (b) to the extent list ("or the whole range if WP-PDR-22 keeps the 8 W ceiling"). In the memo, add a clause such as "if the 8 W ceiling is kept instead, the power may fall short across the whole battery range; that choice also comes to you with the re-baseline change". It can be fixed in place, because ADR-060 is Proposed.
