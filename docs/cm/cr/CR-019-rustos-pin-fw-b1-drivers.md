## 2. Reason

The FW-B1 minimal keyer set (RSK-013 step list; plan WP-PDR-41 objective) is five rustos work packages, reviewed to APPROVED at iteration 2 by the independent code reviewer and software assurance (INSP-095 to INSP-099 and INSP-102 to INSP-106). The record verdicts are held until the owner's merge and the pin-move CR bring the reviewed blobs into a configuration cwht consumes (lead SE convention of 2026-09-27, stated in each record's front matter; with no merge before the pin, the owner's OD-37 disposition of this CR takes the place of the merge, and the hold comment is restated by a section 4 cross item; fix round 2, SA2-F1). The cwht firmware builds against the commit the lock names (05 Table 4-1 rows 26 and 27; `docs/plan/schedule.md` section 2, the RSK-023 control). Until the pin moves, no cwht build can use the drivers, and the keyer prototype image with PWM sidetone for WP-PDR-40 cannot be built (plan section 6.2 PCR-4 row, "Dependent products": keyer prototype image, bench session, lock at F1). None of the sprint records SW-01 to SW-05 ran `tools/sw_gate.sh`, because no agent reads the owner's rustos working tree (rule C3); each says "The gate runs on the pin-move CR branch ... against the merged rustos commit". This CR is that run, against the pinned reviewed commit `48e07ec` (fix round 2). Workaround while the CR is open: none; the cwht firmware stays on `2ec64c0` and the FW-B0 heartbeat image.

ADR-055 gives the driver full-scale on and off with no envelope or level control; the HZ-005 firmware controls (level cap, limiter, envelope) are allocated to SW-AUDIO, which does not exist yet. So the prototype image carries no HZ-005 firmware control, and a GPIO square wave into headphones is far above the 150 mVrms into 32 ohm budget for any digital input (HZ-005 K1, K7; RSK-017 mitigation step S1; REQ-SYS-072). Condition **H1**, carried into K7, the prototype record and the WP-PDR-40 bench procedure: before the owner listens, the sidetone path is bounded by hardware that does not depend on the firmware, by one of two arrangements. (a) A small speaker or piezo at the bench, with no headphones on the sidetone output. (b) A fixed passive attenuator between the PWM pin and the headphone jack, whose output is checked before headphones are worn (fix round 2, IR2-F2 with the section 6.5 additions). The level bound is the 30 mVrms default cap of REQ-SYS-074 (the 85 dB(A) class, NGO-016), which the product itself holds until the operator acknowledges a high-level warning. It is not the 150 mVrms worst-pattern ceiling of REQ-SYS-072, which no pattern may exceed and which is not a listening level: at 150 mVrms the most sensitive earbuds give about 105 dB SPL (`docs/research/audio-output-and-hearing-safety.md`, R-AUD-3). The check: a 32 ohm resistor in place of the headphones on each channel the headphone plug connects (tip and ring); the sidetone at full scale, meaning the largest AC level the image can produce (for the PWM square wave, 50 percent duty, the largest AC part of a 0 to 3.3 V wave; if the image offers level, duty or frequency settings, the setting that gives the largest reading); the tone frequency inside the band for which the meter's datasheet states its AC accuracy; the owner's Fluke 174 (true RMS, status note 2026-09-28 §2) on its AC voltage range. **Pass: each channel reads at most 25.0 mVrms**, or at most R_max = (0.90 × 30 mVrms − c) / (1 + p) if that is lower, where p is the meter's AC accuracy as a fraction of reading and c its count term in mV at the range resolution, both copied from the datasheet into the record at the tone frequency. The factor 0.90 is the square-wave allowance: a meter whose band passes only the fundamental reads 0.90 of a square wave's RMS, because the fundamental carries 8/π², about 81 percent, of its power. For example, p = 0.02 and c = 0.3 mV give R_max = 26.2 mVrms, so 25.0 mVrms applies; a reading of 25.0 mVrms then bounds the true level at about 28.7 mVrms. The check is repeated after any change to the attenuator, the output pin or the image. It is a safety precondition, not credited evidence: the meter has no TV record yet (OQ-VV-003) and 04 §6.3 applies to credited use, so the record says "safety check, not credited". A sidetone louder than this cap is not part of this CR; it would need the owner's own ruling. The prototype record and `docs/vv/reports/devcheck-keyer-hsi-r1.md` state the arrangement used and, for (b), the datasheet values p and c, the tone frequency, the setting used and the reading on each channel.
