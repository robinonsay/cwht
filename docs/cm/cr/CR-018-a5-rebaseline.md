## 2. Reason

Origin: the owner's choice of A5 on 2026-09-29 (TS-012 revision 7 section 10; status note 2026-09-29 sections 4 and 5; SI-044) and the owner inputs of 2026-09-27 that TS-012 was restarted on (SI-040 to SI-043). TS-012 section 10 condition 2 routes the A5 requirement deltas to this CR; PDR work plan revision 6 WP-PDR-53 defines its scope and its disposition, OD-40 at S1. The functional baseline `baseline/srr` describes a PCBWay turnkey radio with a display, two encoders, an in-radio USB charger with three protection layers and a four-layer board; the owner has replaced each of these. Without this CR the architecture, the L2 files and the TBR sheet (WP-PDR-31 to 36, 45) would be written against requirements that the chosen design fails by construction (a display it does not have, a charger it does not carry, turnkey placement nobody performs), and every A5 product would need a waiver.

Workaround while the CR is open: the PDR products are drafted on this CR's text and marked AT RISK (A5 CRs) until the S1 disposition (PDR work plan rules C8 and C13); nothing is ordered before the ordering gate of TS-012 section 8.4, which follows CDR.
