## 1. Description of the change

### 1.0 The reading this CR implements

1. **First build (revision 3).** JLCPCB fabricates five bare boards of each of the two A5 board designs, a main board and an RF board, each within 100 x 100 mm (TS-012 section 8.10, REQ-SYS-139 row; five pieces per design is the JLCPCB standard order, TS-012 section 8.11 item 5). The owner assembles one complete unit, `CWHT-A-001`, from one board of each design (SI-040, SI-041), in the enclosure that CR-003 accepts for it. The four remaining boards of each design are kept bare; one spare board serves as the FR4 end wall that faces the heat sink (CR-003 revision 4; TS-012 design item D-2). Revision 2's PCBWay order (five fabricated, one assembled by PCBWay) is superseded by the owner inputs of 2026-09-27 (SI-040 to SI-042) and the A5 choice.
2. **Further units.** Whether any further unit is built is an owner decision taken after the first unit has been evaluated. This CR proposes the decision point (section 12 Q4) and the options (section 3.2), and it decides nothing about further units.
3. **Unchanged.** The cap of at most five complete units across every revision, none marketed (REQ-SYS-125, 47 CFR 15.23(a)), stays. The intent to hand radios to friends (SI-019) stays: a friend can operate `CWHT-A-001` as their own station (OPS-006, operator model OPS-A), and further units remain possible within the cap. The acceptance test procedure of `docs/process/04-verification-and-validation.md` §11.1 stays a CDR product. Line 83 of 04 already admits a friend's radio as the second station of OnAir runs, so 04 does not change.
4. **What changes.** ADR-025 and SI-035 set five boards fabricated and three assembled, and SRR decision 86 kept three as the planning value with up to five decided at CDR. SRR decision 90 amortizes a USD 610 unit budget over three units. After this CR the first build is one unit assembled by the owner, the cost ceiling is the owner's USD 300 maximum for that first unit with USD 200 as the target (SI-042; revision 3), and every product that divides by three units, or needs a second cwht unit, is restated.
5. **First build, defined (revision 3).** The first build is the first JLCPCB order at the CDR procurement release (`HW-MB-revA-1`) and the one unit the owner assembles from it and puts into an enclosure. The bare boards of the same order are not complete units.

## 2. Reason
