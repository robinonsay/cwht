# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this topic is to provide a list of all the downloadable Checklists (a.k.a. Process Asset Templates (PATs)) contained in this SWE Handbook. The PAT Checklists provide the basics for assessing and evaluating software engineering project processes and products for compliance with an established set of criteria.  They are useful to anyone needing to provide an independent assessment of compliance with a set of defined processes, standards, requirements, etc. In most cases, the applicable SWE Requirement or Topic will be listed in the description of each PAT.

The PAT Checklists on each tab pertain to the specified topic area. The topic areas are:

* [Assessments](/spaces/SITE/pages/200212502/Assessment+Checklists+Full+Detail) — Checklists for verifying that software engineering documents meet the Minimum Recommended Content guidance.
* [Audits](/spaces/SITE/pages/114327650/Audit+Checklists+Full+Detail) — Checklists for verifying compliance with the **NPR 7150.2 SWE Requirements** [083](#_tabs-<p>23</p>) and processes and procedures.
* [Change Management](/spaces/SITE/pages/114327655/Change+Management+Full+Detail) — Checklists for auditing and assessing Change Management processes, procedures, and documents.
* [Coding Practices](/spaces/SITE/pages/204079217/Coding+Practices+Full+Detail) — Checklists for reviewing source code.
* [Commercial and Legacy Software](/spaces/SITE/pages/204079221/Commercial+and+Legacy+Software+Full+Detail) —  Checklists for selecting commercial and legacy software. (Same as OTS.)
* [Configuration Management](/spaces/SITE/pages/114327658/Configuration+Management+Full+Detail) — Checklists for auditing and assessing Configuration Management processes, procedures, and documents.
* [Design Analysis](/spaces/SITE/pages/114327672/Design+Design+Analysis+Full+Detail) — Checklists for auditing, assessing, and analyzing a software design at its various stages of the design life cycle.
* [Hazard Analysis](/spaces/SITE/pages/114327676/Hazard+Analysis+Full+Detail) — Checklists applicable to hazards throughout the life cycle.
* [Implementation](/spaces/SITE/pages/114327674/Implementation+Full+Detail) — Checklists for auditing, assessing, and analyzing source code during the implementation phase.
* [Maintenance and Operations](/spaces/SITE/pages/114327678/Maintenance+Operations+Full+Detail) — Checklists for auditing and assessing Maintenance and Operations processes, procedures, and documents.
* [Milestone Reviews](/spaces/SITE/pages/205029611/Milestone+Reviews+Full+Detail) — Checklists for assessing review success criteria.
* [Off-The-Shelf Software](/spaces/SITE/pages/114327669/COTS+MOTS+GOTS+OS+Reuse+SW+Legacy+SW+Full+Detail) — Checklists for choosing COTS, GOTS, MOTS, OSS, Reuse Software, and Legacy Software.
* [Peer Reviews](/spaces/SITE/pages/114327682/Peer+Reviews+Full+Detail) — Checklists for performing peer reviews throughout the life cycle.
* [Planning Process](/spaces/SITE/pages/204079225/Planning+Process+Full+Detail) — Checklists for auditing, assessing, and analyzing the various plans and artifacts required for project planning and management.
* [Release and Delivery](/spaces/SITE/pages/114327684/Release+and+Delivery+Assets+Full+Detail) — Checklists for auditing and assessing software releases and deliveries.
* [Requirements Development and Analysis](/spaces/SITE/pages/114327686/Requirements+Development+Analysis+Full+Detail) — Checklists for auditing, assessing, and analyzing software requirements.
* [Risk Management](/spaces/SITE/pages/204079227/Risk+Management+Full+Detail) — Checklists for assessing the Risk Management Plan.
* [Safety Specific](/spaces/SITE/pages/114327688/Safety+Specific+Full+Detail) — Checklists for activities associated with safety-critical software.
* [SA Tasking](/spaces/SITE/pages/204079229/Software+Assurance+Tasking+Full+Detail) — Checklists for performing software assurance tasking.
* [Testing and Test Analysis](/spaces/SITE/pages/114327690/Testing+and+Test+Analysis+Full+Detail) — Checklists for reviewing test plans and procedures and analyzing their content.
* [Test Results and Documentation](/spaces/SITE/pages/204079231/Test+Results+and+Documentation+Full+Detail) — Checklists for auditing, assessing, and analyzing test documentation.
